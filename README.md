Digital Payments in Morocco: Evolution and Impact on Financial Inclusion

1. Introduction

Digital payments are becoming increasingly important in the Moroccan financial sector. They offer new ways to make transactions through bank cards, mobile applications and electronic platforms.

2. Research Question

To what extent does the development of digital payments contribute to improving financial inclusion in Morocco?

3. Objectives

- Understand the development of digital payments in Morocco.
- Identify the main digital payment methods.
- Analyze their impact on financial inclusion.
- Identify the main challenges facing digital payments.

4. Methodology

This study is based on secondary data collected from official reports and public sources, particularly Bank Al-Maghrib and the World Bank.

The analysis will use tables, indicators and graphical representations.

5. Expected Results

The study aims to identify the opportunities and challenges of digital payments and evaluate their contribution to financial inclusion in Morocco.
